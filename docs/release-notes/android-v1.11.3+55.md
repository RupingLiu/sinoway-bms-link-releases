## English

- Cloud device data now includes the battery operating state, displayed as charging, discharging, or idle across Overview, Devices, and Device Detail.
- Overview and Devices now share the same device-status layout, with a consistent SOC, connection, battery-state, and health scan order.
- Long device identifiers, board models, and customer/site text stay complete and readable instead of truncating or wrapping because of limited card width.
- The Device Detail status page no longer exposes the SN and shows the full customer and site information.
