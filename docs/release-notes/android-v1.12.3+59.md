## English

- Refreshed interface: metrics use large tabular numerals and the type scale is rebalanced for a calmer, more premium look.
- Status indicators are now dot + text; colored icon tiles and pill badges are removed. Cards drop their outlines with larger corner radius, and the bottom bar loses its selection pill.
- Overview metric cards drop redundant subtitles; the device detail status tab becomes an instrument-style big-number layout.
- The company wordmark is back in the app bar and as a footer on the Profile page, in the new dark red/green version.
- Map actions become text-only buttons with hairline separators, and the map attribution moves to the lower-left corner.
- Profile account card rebuilt: role, customer scope, and device count merge into one line, with sign-out as a centered red action below a divider.
- Device and alarm list icons are vertically centered against multi-line content.
